Lane Defense

Plant defenders in five lanes and stop the march.

▶ Play Lane Defense now All games

How to play Lane Defense

  1. Tap a defender card (or drag it) and then a lawn tile to plant it. Tap glowing energy orbs to collect them.
  2. Glow Blooms make energy, Pea Pods shoot up their lane, Rock Nuts block, Boom Berries blast a 3×3 area and Frost Pods slow monsters.
  3. Monsters march down the lanes. A mower saves each lane once — if a monster gets past it, the run ends. Each level ends with a huge final wave; every 5th level brings Giant bosses.
  4. Shovel digs up a defender for half its cost; Lightning zaps every monster. Upgrades add starting energy, damage and coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Survive 5 waves
  • Defeat 150 monsters in total
  • Reach level 3
  • Survive 15 waves
  • Defeat a Giant boss
  • Defeat 2,000 monsters in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Lane Defense:

  • ☀️ Morning Sun — +25 starting energy each level
  • 🌱 Strong Roots — +10% damage for all defenders
  • 🪙 Garden Market — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
