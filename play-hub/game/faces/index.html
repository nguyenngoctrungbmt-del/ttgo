Face Names

Meet the characters, remember their names, call them out.

▶ Play Face Names now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Face Names

  1. Guests step into the spotlight one by one and say their name — tap to meet the next one.
  2. Then find them: tap the guest who was named, or pick the right name, job or favourite colour for a face.
  3. Wrong answers cost a heart. Strangers and look-alikes join the line-up, and guests from earlier levels come back.
  4. Use a Name Badge hint to reveal tags or remove two wrong answers. Every 5th level is a Reunion bonus round.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 4
  • Recall 30 guests
  • Get a 6-answer streak
  • Reach level 10
  • Recognise 15 returning guests
  • Recall 300 guests

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Face Names:

  • ⏳ Good Look — +15% time to meet each guest
  • 🏷️ Name Badges — Start each run with +1 hint
  • ❤️ Big Heart — +1 max heart

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
