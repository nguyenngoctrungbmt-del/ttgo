Flood It

Flood the board one color at a time before moves run out.

▶ Play Flood It now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Flood It

  1. The flood starts from the top-left cell (subtle inset).
  2. Pick a color to recolor the whole connected region from that corner.
  3. Fill the board with one color before moves run out.
  4. Levels are endless — the grid grows and moves tighten as you go.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–3 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
