Dogfight Ace

Out-turn enemy fighters, dodge missiles and down the enemy aces.

▶ Play Dogfight Ace now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Dogfight Ace

  1. Drag anywhere to steer — your fighter always flies forward and its guns fire automatically at anything in front of the nose.
  2. Shoot down every enemy in the wave. Red arrows on the screen edge point to planes out of view.
  3. When MISSILE LOCK flashes, tap FLARE to break the lock, or turn hard to out-run the missile. Every 5th wave an enemy ace attacks.
  4. Pick an upgrade after each wave, grab wrenches to repair and flare crates to restock. Buy permanent upgrades between runs.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach wave 3
  • Shoot down 40 planes in total
  • Evade 3 enemy missiles
  • Reach wave 6
  • Shoot down 2 enemy aces
  • Shoot down 30 planes in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Dogfight Ace:

  • 🛡️ Armored Hull — +1 max hull point
  • 🎆 Flare Racks — +1 flare every wave
  • 🔫 Gun Tuning — Guns fire 10% faster

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
