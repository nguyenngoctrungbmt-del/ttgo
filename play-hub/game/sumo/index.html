Sumo Push

Shove, charge and sidestep rivals out of a shrinking sumo ring.

▶ Play Sumo Push now All games

How to play Sumo Push

  1. Drag anywhere to move your wrestler. Hold your finger still to build a charge, release to dash at your rival.
  2. Push your rival out of the ring to win the bout — best of 3 wins the match. The ring shrinks the longer a bout lasts.
  3. Rivals have weight and tricks: pushers, heavyweights, chargers that glow before dashing, and sidesteppers that dodge your charge.
  4. 20 rivals in 8 venues — every 5th is a Yokozuna with a signature move. Stars: win, 2–0 sweep, a charge win. Replay beaten rivals from the map.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Win 3 bouts
  • Beat your first rival
  • Win 5 bouts with a charge
  • Reach the 4th rival
  • Win 5 matches 2–0
  • Win 40 bouts in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Sumo Push:

  • 🍙 Heavyweight — +10% body mass — harder to shove
  • ⚡ Thunder Charge — Charge fills faster and hits 15% harder
  • 🦶 Iron Feet — Dig in harder at the edge of the ring

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–7 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
