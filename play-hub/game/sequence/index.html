How to play Pulse Sequence

  1. Press Start and watch the glowing pads carefully.
  2. Repeat the exact sequence by tapping the pads.
  3. Each round adds one more step to the pattern.
  4. Reach level 12 to master the game, or miss once to end.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–3 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
