Bounce Up

Bounce from meadow to outer space on springs, hats and jetpacks.

▶ Play Bounce Up now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Bounce Up

  1. You bounce automatically. Drag left or right (or hold a side of the screen) to steer; the screen wraps around.
  2. Climb as high as you can. Brown platforms crumble, blue ones move, white ones vanish after one bounce. Falling off the bottom ends the run.
  3. Stomp monsters from above or tap to shoot them; touching them from below hurts. Spiky ones can only be shot.
  4. Springs, trampolines, propeller hats, jetpacks and shields boost you. Upgrades give a rocket start, shields and more power-ups.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Climb 500 m in one run
  • Bounce on 15 springs
  • Defeat 5 monsters
  • Climb 1,500 m in one run
  • Use 15 power-ups
  • Stomp 30 monsters

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Bounce Up:

  • 🚀 Rocket Start — Start with a jetpack (+1 s per level)
  • 🛡️ Bubble Shield — Start with +1 shield bubble
  • 🍀 Lucky Finds — +20% springs and power-ups

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
