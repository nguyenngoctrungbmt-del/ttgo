All in Hole

Steer the hole and swallow every last item in the level.

▶ Play All in Hole now Get the app on Google Play

How to play All in Hole

  1. Drag anywhere (or use the arrow keys) to slide the hole across the table.
  2. Anything smaller than the hole tips in. The hole grows as it eats — swallow 100% before time runs out. Faster clears earn more stars.
  3. Walls block the hole, moving platforms shove things around, and swallowing a bomb costs 6 seconds.
  4. Magnet pulls nearby items in, Mega Hole grows it for a moment, Freeze stops the clock. Upgrades give a bigger hole and more time.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Swallow 300 items in total
  • Earn 6 stars in one run
  • Reach level 7
  • Swallow 30 in one streak
  • Swallow 3,000 items in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for All in Hole:

  • ⭕ Wide Mouth — +6% starting hole size
  • ⏱️ Extra Time — +5 seconds on every level
  • 🪙 Lost & Found — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
