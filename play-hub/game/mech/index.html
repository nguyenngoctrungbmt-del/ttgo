Mech Survivor

Survive 10 minutes of swarms while your auto-weapons level and evolve.

▶ Play Mech Survivor now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Mech Survivor

  1. Drag anywhere (or WASD / arrows) to walk your mech. Weapons aim and fire on their own.
  2. Collect XP gems to level up, then pick a new weapon, a weapon level or a passive part.
  3. A max-level weapon plus its matching part evolves when you open a chest — elites drop chests every minute.
  4. Dodge mortar circles, tesla beams and meteor showers. Bosses arrive at 5:00 and 10:00 — beat the Overlord to win, then try endless mode and the Colossus.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Survive 3 minutes
  • Destroy 500 enemies in total
  • Reach mech level 10
  • Defeat the 5-minute Warden
  • Evolve a weapon
  • Destroy 1,000 enemies in a run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Mech Survivor:

  • 🛡️ Reinforced Hull — +15 max hull HP
  • 💥 Power Core — +8% weapon damage
  • 🧲 Tractor Beam — +20% gem pickup radius

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 5–12 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
