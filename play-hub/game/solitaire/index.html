Klondike

Classic solitaire — build foundations from ace to king.

▶ Play Klondike now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Klondike

  1. Tap the stock to flip cards onto the waste pile.
  2. Build tableau columns down in alternating colors; move face-up runs together.
  3. Foundations build up by suit from Ace to King — empty columns take Kings only.
  4. Clear all four foundations to win. Use New deal anytime.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 5–15 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
