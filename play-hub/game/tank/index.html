Tank Arena

Drive, auto-fire, and survive waves of slimes and bosses.

▶ Play Tank Arena now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Tank Arena

  1. Touch and drag anywhere to drive. Your turret aims at the nearest enemy and fires on its own.
  2. Slimes chase you: runners are fast, brutes are tanky, spitters shoot from range, splitters divide in two.
  3. Clear a wave to pick an upgrade — heavier shells, twin barrels, piercing, explosive rounds and more.
  4. Every 5th wave brings a crowned boss — dodge its ring volley when it swells. Coins buy permanent armor, loader and repair upgrades.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Destroy 50 enemies in one run
  • Reach wave 4
  • Destroy 300 enemies in total
  • Defeat a boss
  • Reach wave 7
  • Score 3,000 in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Tank Arena:

  • 🛡️ Plating — +1 max HP at the start of each run
  • ⚙️ Quick Loader — +10% base fire rate
  • 🔧 Repair Kit — Heal 1 HP every few waves (more often per level)

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
