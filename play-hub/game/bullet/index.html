Bullet Storm

Weave through dazzling bullet storms, graze for points, slay bosses.

▶ Play Bullet Storm now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Bullet Storm

  1. Drag anywhere to fly — the ship follows your finger’s movement and fires automatically. Only the tiny glowing core can be hit.
  2. 20 scripted stages, each ending in a boss — a grand boss every 5th with walls, webs, heartbeat rings and lasers. Stars: clear, no miss, break every phase in time.
  3. Bullets that brush past the core are grazes — they score points and every 50 grazes earns a bomb. Tap BOMB to wipe the screen in a pinch.
  4. Collect red P chips to raise your power level and blue chips for points. Fly near the top to vacuum up every item.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach stage 2
  • Graze 150 bullets in total
  • Defeat a boss
  • Graze 120 bullets in one run
  • Reach stage 4
  • Defeat 10 bosses

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Bullet Storm:

  • 💣 Bomb Bay — +1 bomb at the start and after each loss
  • ⚡ Overcharge — Start with +1 power level
  • 🧲 Item Magnet — Bigger pickup radius, lower vacuum line

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
