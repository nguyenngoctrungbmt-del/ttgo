Samurai Parry

Read the strike, parry on the beat, and cut down every challenger.

▶ Play Samurai Parry now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Samurai Parry

  1. Foes walk up and wind up a strike — a ring shrinks around your blade.
  2. Tap as the ring closes: dead-on is a PERFECT counter. Too early and you stumble; red rings are feints.
  3. Foes soon come from both sides — tap that half. Ninjas throw stars, a Shogun comes every 10 kills.
  4. Spend coins on upgrades: extra hearts, a wider PERFECT window and bonus coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Defeat 10 foes in one run
  • Land 5 perfect parries in one run
  • Defeat 100 foes in total
  • Chain 8 perfect parries
  • Defeat a Shogun
  • Score 150 in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Samurai Parry:

  • ❤️ Iron Will — +1 max heart
  • 🎯 Focus — +15% wider PERFECT window
  • 🪙 Bounty — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–4 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
