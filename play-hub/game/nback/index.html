N-Back Flash

Is this the same as N steps back? Train your working memory.

▶ Play N-Back Flash now All games

How to play N-Back Flash

  1. Shapes (or lit squares) flash one at a time. Tap MATCH (or press Space) when the current one is the same as the one N steps back.
  2. Start with 1-back, then 2-back and 3-back. Later levels use grid positions, then shape and position at once with two buttons (Space / L).
  3. A wrong MATCH or a missed match costs a heart. Accuracy decides your score and stars; hit streaks build a combo.
  4. The history strip helps early and fades as you improve. Peek shows it again and Slow calms the stream. Upgrades add hearts and slow every level.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 4
  • Catch 30 matches in total
  • Finish a level with 100%
  • Reach level 8
  • Clear a 2-back level
  • Catch 10 matches in a row

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for N-Back Flash:

  • 🧘 Calm Mind — The stream runs 7% slower
  • ❤️ Big Heart — +1 max heart
  • 🧠 Focus Kit — +1 starting Peek and Slow

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
