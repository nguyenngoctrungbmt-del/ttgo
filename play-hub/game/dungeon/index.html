Dungeon Dash

Slash through torch-lit rooms, grab perks and topple floor bosses.

▶ Play Dungeon Dash now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Dungeon Dash

  1. Drag anywhere to move. Your sword swings automatically at nearby monsters — or tap the sword button to strike.
  2. Tap the roll button to dodge with a burst of invincibility. Watch for red warnings: archers aim, knights wind up, bosses charge.
  3. Doors lock until a room is clear. Each floor is a level of hand-built rooms ending in a guardian room — every 5th floor holds a boss. Fewer hits earn more stars.
  4. Open chests to pick 1 of 3 perks: more damage, extra hearts, spin attacks, wave slashes, lifesteal and more.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Clear 5 rooms in one run
  • Defeat 100 monsters
  • Defeat a floor boss
  • Reach floor 3
  • Collect 20 perks
  • Defeat 60 monsters in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Dungeon Dash:

  • ❤️ Vigor — +1 max heart
  • ⚔️ Whetstone — +25% sword damage
  • 🎁 Relic Hunter — Start each run with +1 random perk

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
