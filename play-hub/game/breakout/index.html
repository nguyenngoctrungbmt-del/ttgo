Brick Bash

Smash bricks, catch falling power-ups, and dodge bomber foes.

▶ Play Brick Bash now All games

How to play Brick Bash

  1. Slide to move the paddle. Tap once to serve the ball.
  2. Break bricks — power-ups may drop. Catch them: wide, multi-ball, slow, life, fireball, score.
  3. Enemies fly across and drop bombs — dodge or lose a life. Hit enemies with the ball for bonus points.
  4. You have 3 lives. Lose them all and the run restarts from level 1.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
