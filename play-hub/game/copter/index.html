Copter Cave

Hold to rise, release to dip through ice, lava and crystal caves.

▶ Play Copter Cave now All games

How to play Copter Cave

  1. Hold anywhere to lift the helicopter; release to dip. Momentum matters, so feather your touch.
  2. Do not touch the cave walls, floating rocks, crusher pillars, bats or falling stalactites (they shake before they drop).
  3. Fuel drains as you fly. Grab green fuel cans to refill and gems for bonus coins.
  4. The cave narrows and a new biome begins every 600 m. Upgrades give a bigger tank, armor and a gem magnet.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Fly 300 m in one run
  • Collect 50 gems
  • Pick up 5 fuel cans
  • Fly 800 m in one run
  • Get 15 close calls
  • Collect 60 gems in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Copter Cave:

  • ⛽ Big Tank — Fuel drains 12% slower
  • 🛡️ Rotor Armor — Survive +1 crash per run
  • 💎 Gem Magnet — Pull in gems from further away

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
