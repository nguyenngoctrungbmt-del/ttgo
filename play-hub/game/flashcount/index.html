Flash Count

Objects flash for a moment — how many of each did you see?

▶ Play Flash Count now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Flash Count

  1. A burst of apples, stars, gems, critters and more flashes on the table — count each kind before the curtain drops.
  2. Answer "how many?" (or "which was most?") by tapping a big answer button before the timer runs out.
  3. Wrong answers cost a heart; lose all 3 and the show is over. Streaks raise your score multiplier and earn Peeks.
  4. Tap the eye to peek once more. Objects start moving, overlapping and arriving late — every 5th level is a bonus round.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 4
  • Answer 25 questions right
  • Get a 5-answer streak
  • Reach level 10
  • Spot the "most" kind 10 times
  • Answer 250 questions right

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Flash Count:

  • ⏳ Long Look — +15% flash time on every level
  • 👁️ Third Eye — Start each run with +1 Peek
  • 🛡️ Lucky Charm — +1 shield that forgives a wrong answer

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
