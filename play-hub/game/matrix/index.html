Grid Recall

Memorise the lit tiles, then tap them back as the grid grows.

▶ Play Grid Recall now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Grid Recall

  1. Watch the grid: some tiles flip up for a moment. Then they flip back — tap every one you saw.
  2. A wrong tile costs a heart. Lose all hearts and the run ends. Perfect rounds build a streak multiplier.
  3. The grid grows from 3×3 to 7×7. New twists appear: tiles in order, red decoys to ignore, and a grid that rotates.
  4. Use Peek to re-show the tiles and Hint to reveal one. Upgrades give a longer preview, extra hearts and more power-ups.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 5
  • Recall 100 tiles in total
  • Play 10 perfect rounds
  • Reach level 10
  • Get a 5-round perfect streak
  • Clear 15 in-order rounds

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Grid Recall:

  • 👁️ Focus — +0.3 s to every memorise phase
  • ❤️ Big Heart — +1 max heart
  • 🔍 Toolkit — +1 starting Peek and Hint

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
