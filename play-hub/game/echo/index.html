Pattern Echo

Find the color sequence on the grid, in order.

▶ Play Pattern Echo now All games

How to play Pattern Echo

  1. Study the color swatches at the top.
  2. Tap matching colors on the grid in the same order.
  3. Wrong colors cost a life and reshuffle the board.
  4. Longer patterns appear as you climb levels.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–3 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
