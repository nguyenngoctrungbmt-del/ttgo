Rock Blaster

Fly through an asteroid field, shatter rocks, and outgun UFOs.

▶ Play Rock Blaster now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Rock Blaster

  1. Touch and drag anywhere — the ship turns and thrusts toward your drag. It fires on its own.
  2. Big rocks split into smaller, faster rocks. Small rocks are worth the most.
  3. From wave 2, UFOs fly by and shoot back. Down them for +300 and a power-up.
  4. Power-ups: 🔱 triple shot, ⚡ rapid fire, 🛡️ shield. Crystal rocks drop two. From wave 4, dodge comets along the red warning lines.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Destroy 40 rocks in one run
  • Reach wave 3
  • Destroy 200 rocks in total
  • Score 1,500 in one run
  • Reach wave 5
  • Shoot down 3 UFOs

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Rock Blaster:

  • 🚀 Spare Ships — +1 ship at the start of each run
  • 🔫 Overclock — +12% fire rate
  • 🔋 Power Cells — Power-ups last 25% longer and drop more

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
