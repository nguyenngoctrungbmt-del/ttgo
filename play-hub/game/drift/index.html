Drift King

One-touch drifting: hold into the bend, hit apex gates, stay on track.

▶ Play Drift King now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Drift King

  1. Your car drives itself. Hold anywhere to drift into the next bend, release to straighten out.
  2. Drift through the glowing apex gates to raise your multiplier — drift points bank when you straighten.
  3. Rumble strips reset your multiplier and leaving the track is a crash. Later come cones, oil, rolling barrels and spark mines — grab nitro to smash through.
  4. Checkpoints speed you up and change scenery; overtake rival ghosts for +500 and survive Drift Frenzy for big bonuses. Upgrades: Wide Track, Tow Truck, Head Start.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Clear 10 corners in one run
  • Drift through 15 apex gates
  • Pass 8 checkpoints in total
  • Clear 30 corners in one run
  • Reach a x6 multiplier
  • Overtake 3 rival ghosts

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Drift King:

  • 🛣️ Wide Track — Track is 6% wider per level
  • 🚚 Tow Truck — Get towed back after 1 crash per run
  • ✖️ Head Start — Start every run with +1 multiplier

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
