Block Blitz

Slide, spin and slam glossy blocks — chain combos as speed climbs.

▶ Play Block Blitz now All games

How to play Block Blitz

  1. Drag left/right to slide the piece, tap to rotate (tap the far left edge to rotate back), flick down to slam it, flick up or tap HOLD to save it.
  2. Fill a whole row to clear it. Clear 4 at once for a QUAD, chain clears for combos, and land back-to-back quads or T-spins for ×1.5.
  3. Every 10 lines the level rises: pieces fall faster and the theme changes. Watch for rising garbage — the stack must not reach the top.
  4. Gems in blocks pay bonus points and coins. Tap BOMB to blast the bottom 3 rows. Upgrades slow gravity, add bombs and find more gems.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Clear 10 lines in one run
  • Reach level 3
  • Clear 100 lines in total
  • Clear 3 QUADs (4 lines)
  • Reach level 6
  • Chain a 4× combo

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Block Blitz:

  • 🐢 Calm Gravity — Pieces fall 10% slower
  • 💣 Line Bomb — Start each run with +1 bomb
  • 💎 Gem Finder — +5% gem chance, +15% coins

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
