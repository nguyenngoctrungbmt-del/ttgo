Chess

Play classic chess against a simple CPU opponent.

▶ Play Chess now All games

How to play Chess

  1. You play white. Tap a piece, then tap a highlighted square to move.
  2. Capture the black king’s position by checkmate to win.
  3. Legal moves only — including castling and pawn promotion to queen.
  4. The CPU replies after each of your moves.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 5–15 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
