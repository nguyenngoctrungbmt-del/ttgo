Rocket Builder

Bolt together a rocket, launch, dodge the sky and reach the Moon.

How to play Rocket Builder

  1. In the hangar, tap or drag parts onto your rocket: engines, fuel tanks, nose cones, fins and side boosters. Watch the thrust, fuel and stability meters.
  2. Launch and drag left or right to steer. Dodge birds, planes, balloons, satellites and meteors; grab coins and fuel cans.
  3. When the boosters run dry, tap STAGE to drop them. Dropping right on time is a perfect staging bonus.
  4. Each flight has a target altitude: miss it and you lose a heart. Cash from every flight buys better parts. Coins buy seed funding, a tougher hull and bonus coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach 1 km
  • Hit 3 flight targets in a run
  • Collect 100 coins in flight
  • Reach the stratosphere (25 km)
  • Make 10 perfect stagings
  • Hit 25 flight targets

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Rocket Builder:

  • 💵 Seed Funding — +40 starting cash per run
  • 🛡️ Tough Hull — +1 hit your rocket can take
  • 🪙 Sponsors — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 4–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
