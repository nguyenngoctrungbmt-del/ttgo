Road Hopper

Hop across roads, rivers and rails — and never stop moving.

▶ Play Road Hopper now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Road Hopper

  1. Tap to hop forward. Swipe left, right or down to hop that way. Trees and rocks block your path.
  2. Cars, trucks and trains squash you — trains are fast, so watch for the flashing red signal. In rivers, land on logs or lily pads.
  3. The camera keeps creeping forward. Lag behind and the eagle snatches you, and logs that drift off-screen take you with them.
  4. Score = furthest row. Every 40 rows brings a new biome; later come snakes, lava vents, bubble shields and Gold Rush lanes full of coins. Upgrades slow the camera, magnet coins and add luck.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Hop 30 rows in one run
  • Collect 20 coins
  • Cross 50 road lanes
  • Hop 100 rows in one run
  • Cross 60 river lanes
  • Collect 200 coins

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Road Hopper:

  • 🦅 Lazy Eagle — Camera creeps 10% slower
  • 🧲 Coin Magnet — Grab coins 1 tile further away
  • 🍀 Lucky Hop — More coins on the road, +15% coins

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
