Jetpack Rush

Hold to blast through zappers, homing missiles and lasers.

▶ Play Jetpack Rush now All games

How to play Jetpack Rush

  1. Hold anywhere to fire the jetpack and rise; release to fall. Fly as far as you can.
  2. Avoid electric zappers, missiles (watch the red warning on the right) and wall lasers that charge before they fire.
  3. Grab coins and power-ups: shield bubble, coin magnet and rocket boost that smashes through zappers.
  4. New hazards appear every 30 s and the zone changes every 500 m. Spend coins on permanent upgrades.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Fly 300 m in one run
  • Collect 150 coins in total
  • Grab 3 power-ups
  • Fly 1,000 m in one run
  • Dodge 30 missiles
  • Get 10 close calls in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Jetpack Rush:

  • 🛡️ Spare Shield — Start each run with +1 shield bubble
  • 🧲 Coin Magnet — Wider coin pickup, magnet lasts +2 s
  • 🚀 Head Start — Rocket through the first +100 m

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
