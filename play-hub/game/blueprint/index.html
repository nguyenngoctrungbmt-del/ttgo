Blueprint

Fill the blueprint with pieces off the belt before time runs out.

▶ Play Blueprint now All games

How to play Blueprint

  1. Drag pieces off the conveyor belt and drop them on the blueprint. They snap into place when every cell fits.
  2. Fill the whole outline before the timer ends. A wrong drop costs 3 seconds; quick clean placements build a combo.
  3. From blueprint 6 tap a piece to rotate it; from blueprint 11 colours must match the plan, and big pieces arrive at 21. Gold stars fit anywhere, clocks add time.
  4. 32 hand-drawn plans, a big build every 5th. Finish fast with no mistakes for 3 stars; replay any plan from the level map.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Finish 3 blueprints in a run
  • Place 60 pieces
  • Earn 12 stars
  • Finish 6 blueprints in a run
  • Finish 5 builds with no mistakes
  • Reach a x6 placement combo

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Blueprint:

  • ⏱️ Overtime — +4 seconds on every blueprint
  • 🐢 Slow Belt — Conveyor runs 8% slower
  • 🪙 Contractor — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
