Hill Rider

Throttle over wild hills, flip in the air, and never run dry.

▶ Play Hill Rider now All games

How to play Hill Rider

  1. Hold the right side for gas and the left side to brake (arrow keys on desktop).
  2. In the air, gas tilts you back and brake tilts you forward — spin all the way around for backflips and frontflips.
  3. Land on your wheels: if your head hits the ground you crash. Grab red fuel cans before the tank runs dry.
  4. Ride on to the Moon, Frozen Peaks and Lava Badlands: dodge rockfalls and fire vents, grab blue nitro, beat rival Blaze in races. Upgrade Fuel Tank, Engine and Helmet with coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Ride 400 m in one run
  • Land 3 flips
  • Collect 250 coins in total
  • Ride 1,200 m in one run
  • Land 3 flips in one run
  • Reach the Moon

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Hill Rider:

  • ⛽ Fuel Tank — Fuel lasts 20% longer
  • ⚙️ Engine — +8% power and top speed
  • ⛑️ Helmet — Survive 1 head crash per run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
