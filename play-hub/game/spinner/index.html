Spin Tops

Steer your battle top, clash for sparks and knock rivals out of the bowl.

▶ Play Spin Tops now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Spin Tops

  1. Drag anywhere (or WASD / arrows) to steer your top around the bowl.
  2. Ram rivals to drain their spin and knock them over the rim. Last top spinning wins the match.
  3. Every clash and every second drains your own spin — stay away from the edge and grab cyan spin orbs.
  4. Use DASH (J) and SHIELD (K) when the meter fills. 20 authored matches across 6 bowls (lava burns spin!) with a boss every 5th. Stars: win, half spin left, under 45 s.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Win 2 matches in one run
  • Ring out 5 tops
  • Land 100 clashes in total
  • Win 5 matches in one run
  • Defeat a boss top
  • Ring out 40 tops in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Spin Tops:

  • 🌀 Iron Core — +10% max spin
  • ⚖️ Heavy Base — +8% weight — less knockback
  • ⚡ Quick Charge — Special meter fills 20% faster

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
