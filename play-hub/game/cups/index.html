Cup Shuffle

Keep your eye on the ball as the cups whirl faster and faster.

▶ Play Cup Shuffle now All games

How to play Cup Shuffle

  1. Watch which cup hides the gold star ball. The cups drop and the host shuffles them — follow the ball, then tap its cup (or press 1–6).
  2. A wrong cup costs a heart; lose them all and the show is over. Perfect rounds build a streak multiplier.
  3. More cups, faster swaps, two or three balls to track, grey fake balls to avoid and cups that hop up to fool you.
  4. Slow-mo slows the current shuffle; X-ray shows through the cups for a moment. Upgrades slow every shuffle and add hearts.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 5
  • Find 30 balls in total
  • Play 10 perfect rounds
  • Reach level 10
  • Get a 6-round perfect streak
  • Win 5 bonus rounds

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Cup Shuffle:

  • 👁️ Sharp Eye — Every shuffle runs 7% slower
  • ❤️ Big Heart — +1 max heart
  • 🎩 Magic Kit — +1 starting Slow-mo and X-ray

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
