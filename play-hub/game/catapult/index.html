Castle Crash

Fling boulders, topple towers and knock out every guard.

▶ Play Castle Crash now All games

How to play Castle Crash

  1. Drag back from anywhere and release to fling a boulder — the dotted arc shows your launch.
  2. Knock out every goblin guard to clear the level. Spare shots earn bonus points and stars.
  3. 32 hand-built castles: glass shatters, wood splinters, stone takes a beating, red crates explode. Every 5th castle a king hides on top.
  4. New ammo unlocks as you go: tap mid-air to split, slam or detonate. Upgrades add reserve shots, power and a longer aim guide.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Knock out 25 guards
  • Smash 150 blocks in total
  • Reach level 7
  • Earn 3 stars on 5 levels
  • Topple a castle king

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Castle Crash:

  • 🪨 Reserve Pile — +1 spare boulder when a level runs dry
  • 💥 Heavy Hitter — +15% impact and blast damage
  • 🎯 Eagle Eye — Longer dotted aim guide

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
