Rooftop Ninja

Sprint across rooftops — jump gaps, slash guards, deflect arrows.

▶ Play Rooftop Ninja now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Rooftop Ninja

  1. You run automatically. Tap the right side to jump — hold for a higher jump, tap again in the air to double jump.
  2. Tap the left side to slash. Slashing cuts down guards and deflects arrows back at archers.
  3. Stomp enemies from above. Running into one, spikes, or a gap ends the run.
  4. Grab coins and go far — the city speeds up and the sky changes every 500 m. Upgrades add a coin magnet and a shield.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Run 300 m in one run
  • Defeat 8 enemies in one run
  • Collect 100 coins in total
  • Run 800 m in one run
  • Deflect 5 arrows
  • Stomp 10 enemies

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Rooftop Ninja:

  • 🧲 Coin Magnet — Pull in coins from farther away
  • 🛡️ Shadow Guard — Survive 1 hit per run per level
  • 🪙 Bounty — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–4 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
