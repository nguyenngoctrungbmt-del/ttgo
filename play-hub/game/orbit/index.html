Orbit Guard

Swing a shield around your planet and smash incoming meteors.

▶ Play Orbit Guard now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Orbit Guard

  1. Drag anywhere — the shield swings to face your finger around the planet.
  2. Block meteors before they hit. Red ones are fast, purple ones take two hits, gold ones split.
  3. Green ✚ healers restore a heart — let them through instead of blocking them.
  4. Chain blocks for a multiplier; every 20 combo triggers Overdrive. Meteor showers hit from the red arc. Upgrades widen the shield.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Block 40 meteors in one run
  • Reach a 15 combo
  • Block 300 meteors in total
  • Survive 90 seconds
  • Reach a 30 combo
  • Score 1,500 in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Orbit Guard:

  • ❤️ Planet Core — +1 max HP for the planet
  • 🛡️ Wide Shield — Shield arc 8% wider
  • ⚡ Long Overdrive — Overdrive lasts 1.5 s longer

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–4 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
