How to play Crate Push

  1. Swipe on the board or use keyboard arrows to move. You can only push one crate at a time.
  2. Push every crate onto a marked goal tile.
  3. Crates cannot be pulled — think before you shove.
  4. Clear all handcrafted levels for a completion bonus.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 5–15 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
