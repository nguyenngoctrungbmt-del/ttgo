Tower Rush

Build and upgrade towers to hold the gate against endless waves.

▶ Play Tower Rush now All games

How to play Tower Rush

  1. Tap a stone pad and pick a tower: Arrow (fast), Cannon (splash), Frost (slows) or Tesla (chain lightning).
  2. Tap a tower to upgrade it through 3 tiers or sell it. Kills and cleared waves earn gold.
  3. Enemies that reach your gate cost lives: runners are fast, tanks are armored, flyers skip the path and healers mend the horde.
  4. Survive every wave of a map to clear its level — no leaks earns 3 stars. 30 hand-built maps add new foes, scarce pads and limited tower kits, with an Ogre King boss every 5th level.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach wave 5
  • Build 12 towers
  • Defeat 300 enemies in total
  • Defeat the Ogre King
  • Reach wave 12
  • Max out 3 towers

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Tower Rush:

  • 💰 Treasury — +40 starting gold
  • 🏰 Thick Walls — +3 lives at the gate
  • ⚒️ Masonry — +8% damage for every tower

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 4–12 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
