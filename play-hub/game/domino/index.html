Domino Build

Draw a domino trail, tap GO and watch the chain ring every bell.

▶ Play Domino Build now All games

How to play Domino Build

  1. Drag your finger from the start pad to lay a trail of dominoes. Start a new line from any domino to split the chain into two branches.
  2. Tap GO: the dominoes topple one after another. Ring every bell before the chain stops to clear the level.
  3. Walls and water block your trail, red gates open and close on a rhythm, and buttons fire hidden chains. Gems and a short trail earn extra stars.
  4. 32 hand-built tables, a boss table every 5th. A broken chain costs a heart but your trail stays so you can fix it (Undo removes the last stroke).

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Clear 3 levels in a run
  • Ring 10 bells
  • Topple 300 dominoes
  • Clear 7 levels in a run
  • Earn 40 stars
  • Collect 25 gems

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Domino Build:

  • 📦 Big Box — +3 dominoes on every level
  • ❤️ Steady Hand — +1 heart per run
  • 🪙 Showman — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
