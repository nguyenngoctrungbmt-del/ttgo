Lane Rush

Switch lanes at speed and dodge the hazards racing toward you.

▶ Play Lane Rush now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Lane Rush

  1. Swipe or tap left/right to switch between three lanes.
  2. Obstacles rush toward you — avoid crates, blocks, and spikes.
  3. Later waves spawn two hazards at once and move faster.
  4. Each clear hazard raises your score. Survive for a long streak.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–3 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
