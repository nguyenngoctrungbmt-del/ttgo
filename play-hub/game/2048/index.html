How to play Merge 2048

  1. Swipe on the board (or use keyboard arrows) to slide all tiles.
  2. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one.
  3. Each merge adds to your score. Reach 2048 to clear the daily goal.
  4. Plan ahead — the game ends when no moves are left.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
