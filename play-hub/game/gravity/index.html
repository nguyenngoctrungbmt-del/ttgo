Gravity Flip

Tap to flip gravity — run the floor and ceiling past saws and gaps.

▶ Play Gravity Flip now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Gravity Flip

  1. Your robot runs by itself. Tap anywhere to flip gravity and swoop between the floor and the ceiling.
  2. Avoid spikes, saws, drones, laser gates, crushers and rockets. If a gap opens under you, flip before you fall out.
  3. Flip at the last moment to dodge a hazard for a NEAR MISS; chain them for combo bonus orbs. Grab a Phase Core to smash through hazards.
  4. Speed ramps up, new hazards arrive every ~30 s and the zone changes every 500 m. Survive the Sentinel boss for big rewards. Upgrades add shields, a magnet and a gentler ramp.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Run 300 m in one run
  • Collect 40 orbs
  • Flip gravity 200 times
  • Run 1,000 m in one run
  • Get 30 near misses
  • Reach a x5 near-miss combo

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Gravity Flip:

  • 🛡️ Bubble Shield — Survive +1 hit per run
  • 🧲 Orb Magnet — Pull in orbs from further away
  • 🐢 Steady Pace — Speed ramps up 10% slower

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
