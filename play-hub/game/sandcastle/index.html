Sandcastle

Raise towers, dig moats and outlast every bigger tide.

▶ Play Sandcastle now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Sandcastle

  1. Drag a piece from the tray onto the beach (or tap a piece, then tap a column). Sand stacks where you drop it, but loose sand slumps off a column more than 4 above its neighbours.
  2. When the tide meter fills, a wave rolls in from the sea and erodes sand from the bottom up. Your tallest tower must reach the goal line or you lose a heart.
  3. Packed walls soak up wave power, moats (shovel) swallow water and stop crabs, wet buckets harden a whole column. Shells and flags that survive pay bonus points.
  4. Tap seagulls before they steal decorations and flip crabs before they pinch your walls. Every 5th tide is a rogue wave. Coins buy stronger sand, bigger buckets and bonus coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Survive 3 tides
  • Build a tower 5 high
  • Place 150 sand blocks
  • Survive 6 tides
  • Save shells from 25 waves
  • Shoo 20 gulls or crabs

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Sandcastle:

  • 🧱 Packed Sand — Walls take +1 more wave hit
  • 🪣 Big Bucket — +1 piece every tide
  • 🪙 Beachcomber — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
