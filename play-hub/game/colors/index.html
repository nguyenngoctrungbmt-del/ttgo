Color Clash

Tap the ink color — ignore what the word says.

How to play Color Clash

  1. A color name appears, but the text may be painted in another color.
  2. Tap the button that matches the ink color, not the written word.
  3. You have a limited time per round — stay sharp.
  4. Build a streak for bonus score before you miss thrice.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–3 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
