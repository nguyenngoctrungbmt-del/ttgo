Sky Archer

Draw, aim and loose — pop balloons, birds and bullseyes in combos.

▶ Play Sky Archer now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Sky Archer

  1. Drag back anywhere to draw the bow — the dots preview the arrow path. Release to loose.
  2. Complete each level’s goal: pop balloons, shoot birds, hit bullseyes and knock apples off heads. Never hit your friend!
  3. Arrows are limited. Multi-pops, bullseyes, hit streaks and golden targets refill your quiver. Watch the wind flag.
  4. 30 hand-built levels, then endless ones. Earn up to 3 stars by using few arrows. Upgrades add arrows, a longer aim guide and more coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Pop 50 balloons in total
  • Hit 3 bullseyes
  • Reach level 7
  • Pop 3 balloons with one arrow
  • Shoot 5 apples off a head

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Sky Archer:

  • 🏹 Big Quiver — +2 arrows at the start of a run
  • 🎯 Eagle Sight — Longer aiming guide
  • 🪙 Bounty — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
