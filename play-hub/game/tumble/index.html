Tumble Tower

Slide blocks out of the tower and stack them higher.

▶ Play Tumble Tower now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Tumble Tower

  1. Press a block and drag to slide it out of the tower. You can’t take from the top two layers.
  2. Loose blocks glide; snug ones must be pulled slowly. Load-bearing blocks flash red: pull one and the tower falls.
  3. The block then hovers over the top. Tap to drop it on a free slot; PERFECT drops chain combos, sloppy ones make the tower sway.
  4. Tremors, cracked and golden blocks appear as you climb. Glue, X-ray and Slow-mo power-ups drop every 5 moves.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Stack 10 blocks in one run
  • Land 10 perfect drops
  • Stack 100 blocks in total
  • Grow the tower to 18 layers
  • Pull 5 golden blocks
  • Chain 5 perfect drops

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Tumble Tower:

  • ✋ Steady Hands — 10% less wobble when pulling
  • 🧴 Glue Stock — +1 glue per run
  • 🪵 Sanded Blocks — Blocks start 10% looser

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–7 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
