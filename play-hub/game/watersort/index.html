Color Pour

Pour colors until each tube is a single shade.

▶ Play Color Pour now All games

How to play Color Pour

  1. Tap a tube to select it, then tap another to pour.
  2. You can only pour onto the same color, or into an empty tube.
  3. A pour moves the top run of matching color until space runs out.
  4. Sort every tube into one solid color to clear the level.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 5–12 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
