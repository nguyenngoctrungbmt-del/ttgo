Farm Merge

Grow, merge and bake your way through busy farm days.

▶ Play Farm Merge now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Farm Merge

  1. Tap a generator (seed bag, chicken coop, apple tree, flower pot) to spend 1 energy and grow an item. Energy refills over time.
  2. Drag two identical items together to merge them: seed, sprout, wheat… bread, cake. Drag anything to the bin to clear space.
  3. Animals post orders at the top — drag the right item onto a card to deliver it. Fill the day’s order goal before sunset or the run ends.
  4. Orders refund energy and can drop gift boxes and wild stars (upgrade any item). New generators, rain and market rushes arrive on later days.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Finish day 2
  • Merge 40 items
  • Fill 10 orders
  • Finish day 4
  • Bake Bread
  • Fill 100 orders

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Farm Merge:

  • ⚡ Big Barn — +5 max energy
  • ☀️ Sunny Days — Energy refills 10% faster
  • 🪙 Market Stall — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 4–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
