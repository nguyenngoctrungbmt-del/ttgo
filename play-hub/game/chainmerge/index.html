Chain Link

Swipe chains of equal gems — they fuse into one bigger number.

▶ Play Chain Link now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Chain Link

  1. Drag through touching tiles (diagonals too) that show the same number. After two equal tiles you may step up to the double, then keep going.
  2. Release to fuse the whole chain into the last tile: the sum becomes the biggest power of two it reaches. Long chains score bonus points.
  3. Tiles fall and refill from the top. When no two touching tiles match, you are out of moves and the run ends.
  4. Shuffle and Hammer save stuck boards. Stones block the board until a merge breaks them; star tiles join any chain; golden tiles pay coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Make a 256 tile
  • Merge 60 chains in total
  • Link a chain of 6 tiles
  • Make a 2048 tile
  • Break 25 stones in total
  • Score 50,000 in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Chain Link:

  • 🔀 Lucky Shuffle — +1 Shuffle at the start of a run
  • 🔨 Gem Hammer — +1 Hammer at the start of a run
  • 🪙 Treasure — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
