Bow Duel

Out-aim a ladder of rival archers across windy hills.

▶ Play Bow Duel now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Bow Duel

  1. Drag back anywhere to set angle and power, then release to shoot. The dots only show the start of the arc.
  2. Take turns with your rival. Headshots deal double damage, legs less — watch the wind gauge before every shot.
  3. 20 rivals on 8 battlefields — healers, dodgers, wind callers — and a champion every 5th. Stars: win, take ≤1 hit, win in ≤4 shots.
  4. After each win pick a reward: new weapons (axe, javelin) or perks. Upgrades add HP, damage and a longer aim guide.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Win 2 duels in one run
  • Land 5 headshots
  • Win 10 duels in total
  • Win 5 duels in one run
  • Land 3 headshots in one run
  • Defeat a champion

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Bow Duel:

  • ❤️ Tough Skin — +15 max HP every run
  • 🎯 Broadheads — +8% damage on every hit
  • 🦅 Long Sight — Longer dotted aim guide

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
