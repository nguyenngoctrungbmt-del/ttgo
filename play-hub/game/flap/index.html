Sky Hop

Tap to flap through moving pipe gaps in the sky.

▶ Play Sky Hop now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Sky Hop

  1. Tap (or press Space) to flap upward — gravity pulls you down.
  2. Fly through the green gaps. After a few gates, pipes start sliding up and down.
  3. Gaps stay wide enough to fit through — track the opening as it moves.
  4. One crash ends the run — try for a longer streak.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–2 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
