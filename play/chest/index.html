How to play Chest Match

Chest Match is a free online memory card game. The board is filled with closed chests. Tap one chest to peek at what’s inside, then tap a second chest. If the contents match, the pair stays open and counts as a match. If they don’t, both close again—remember what you saw for the next turn.

Clear every pair to finish the round. The HUD tracks moves, matches, and time. Your best run is stored in this browser so you can try to beat it later.

Tips to improve

  • Start by sampling corners and edges so you build a mental map quickly.
  • When you uncover a known partner, match it immediately instead of opening a third unknown chest.
  • Count moves carefully: flipping the same wrong pair twice wastes your score.
  • Play a calm first run to learn the set, then race the clock once you know the icons.
