How to play Tile Up Daily

Tile Up Daily is a free online merge puzzle and number tile puzzle you can play instantly in your browser—no download and no account. Each UTC day shares the same daily puzzle seed so everyone faces the same tile sequence while chasing a personal high score.

Look at the Now / next tray for the current tile and upcoming values. Tap any empty square on the 4×4 board to place the highlighted tile. Plan ahead: keep space open for bigger merges and avoid locking low tiles into corners.

Merges need 3 identical tiles touching (up, down, left, or right—not diagonal). When a group of three or more matching numbers connects, they merge 3 tiles into a higher value with a cascade animation and you earn points. Larger clusters still follow the 3-of-a-kind rule and can chain into bigger numbers.

Use Undo to reverse your last move, or spend your one Clear low tile power-up to remove the smallest tile when the board gets tight. The run ends when every cell is filled and no more placements are possible—then tap New run or Play again to retry today’s challenge.
