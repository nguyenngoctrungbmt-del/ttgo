How to play Color Blink

Color Blink is a free memory pattern game inspired by classic Simon. Tap Start and watch the colored buttons light up in order. When it is your turn, tap the same colors in the same sequence.

Every round you survive adds one more color to the pattern. One wrong tap ends the run—beat your best round and chase a longer sequence.

You can tap the buttons or press keys 1–4 (red, green, blue, yellow).

Tips for a higher score

  • Say the color names out loud while watching—the rhythm helps memory.
  • Group the sequence into chunks of two or three colors instead of one long list.
  • Stay calm on longer rounds; speed matters less than accuracy.
  • Replay mentally once before tapping on tough sequences.

FAQ

Is Color Blink free?

Yes. Color Blink is free to play in your browser with no download.

Does the pattern get harder?

Yes. Each successful round adds one more blinking color to the sequence.

Can I use the keyboard?

Yes. Press 1 for red, 2 for green, 3 for blue, and 4 for yellow.
