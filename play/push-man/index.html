How to play Push Man

Push Man is a free online Sokoban puzzle. Move the player with arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen pad. Walk into a box to push it one square—only if the space beyond is empty. Place every box on a goal tile to win the level.

Use Undo to step back after a bad push, switch levels from the selector, and chase a lower move count on your personal best.

Tips to solve levels

  • Never push a box into a corner unless it is already on a goal.
  • Work backward from goals when a level feels stuck.
  • Keep a clear lane so you can walk around boxes later.
  • One wrong push can block the whole puzzle—undo early.

FAQ

Is Push Man free?

Yes. Push Man is free to play in your browser with no download.

Is this the same as Sokoban?

Yes. Push Man follows the classic Sokoban rules: push crates onto goals without pulling them.
