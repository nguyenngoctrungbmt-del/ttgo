How to play Minesweeper

Minesweeper is a classic logic puzzle. Hidden mines sit on the grid. Tap a square to reveal it. If it is safe, a number shows how many mines touch that square (including diagonals). An empty square auto-opens its neighbors.

Right-click (or long-press on mobile) to place a flag on a suspected mine. When the flags around a number match its value, tap the number to open the rest—called a chord.

Win by revealing every non-mine square. Hit a mine and the run ends. Choose Easy (9×9), Medium (16×16), or Hard (30×16). Your first click is always safe.

Tips to win

  • Start from corners and edges—fewer neighbors means easier deductions.
  • If a 1 touches only one hidden square, that square must be a mine.
  • Flag confidently, then use chord clicks to speed up clearing.
  • When two numbers overlap, compare what they share to find forced safe cells.
