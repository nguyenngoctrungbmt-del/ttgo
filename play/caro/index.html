Caro (Gomoku)

Two players place X and O. First to connect five in a row—horizontal, vertical, or diagonal—wins.

Turn: X

How to play Caro

Caro (also known as Gomoku) is a classic five-in-a-row board game. Players take turns placing marks on empty intersections of the grid. The first player to form an unbroken line of five marks—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally—wins the round.

On this page you can play vs AI (toggle in the HUD) or turn the AI off for a local two-player match on the same device. Tap an empty cell to place your mark. Use Restart anytime for a fresh board.

Tips

  • Build open fours (four in a row with space on both ends)—they are hard to block in one move.
  • Watch for forks: two threats created at once usually force a win.
  • Block your opponent’s four-in-a-row immediately, even if you wanted to extend your own line.
  • Control the center early so your lines can grow in more directions.

Yes—Caro is the common Vietnamese name for the five-in-a-row family of games often called Gomoku.
