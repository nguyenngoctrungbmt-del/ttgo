How to play 2048

2048 is a free online number merge puzzle on a 4×4 grid. Each move slides all tiles in one direction. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one. After every move, a new 2 or 4 appears in a random empty cell.

The classic goal is to create the 2048 tile. You can keep playing afterward to chase 4096, 8192, and beyond. The game ends when the board is full and no merges are possible.

Tips for a higher score

  • Keep your highest tile in a corner and build toward it.
  • Pick one edge as your “spine” and avoid moving away from it.
  • Do not scatter large tiles across the board.
  • When stuck, try to free a row or column before the next spawn.

FAQ

Is 2048 free?

Yes. This 2048 game is free to play in your browser with no download.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Swipe up, down, left, or right on the board to move tiles.

Does my best score save?

Yes. Your best score is stored locally in your browser.
