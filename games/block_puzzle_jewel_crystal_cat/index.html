Tips to keep the board alive

1. Protect flat real estate

Tall spikes and deep holes are the silent killers of block puzzles. Whenever two placements look equal, prefer the one that keeps the top contour flatter. Flat boards accept more piece shapes; jagged boards reject almost everything except the one miracle tile you do not have.

2. Clear with intention, not addiction

Instant line clears feel great, but sometimes leaving a near-complete line open sets up a double or triple clear with the next piece. Glance at the upcoming shapes before you spend a perfect fit on a low-value single clear.

3. Park awkward pieces on the edges

Odd crystal shapes often belong against walls where their jagged sides do less damage. Dumping them into the center can fracture the board into unusable pockets. Edge parking buys time until a complementary piece arrives.

4. Slow down when the stack rises

High stacks shrink your margin for error. Take an extra second, rotate the piece in your head, and only then commit. Block Puzzle Jewel Crystal Cat rewards composure more than speed tapping—especially once jewel boards get crowded.
