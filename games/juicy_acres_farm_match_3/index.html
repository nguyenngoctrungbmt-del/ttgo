Juicy Acres: Farm Match 3

Grandma’s old farm has fallen into ruin — and only you can bring it back. Solve juicy match-3 puzzles, earn stars and turn overgrown fields into the coziest farm in the valley.

Get it on Google Play All TTGO apps

Juicy match-3 puzzles

200 colourful levels with apples, corn, carrots and grapes. Match 4 for a Rocket, an L or T shape for a Bomb, and 5 in a row for a Rainbow Ball — then combine power-ups for board-clearing combos.

Restore and build the farm

Spend stars on farm tasks: clear weeds, fix the roof, build a chicken coop, cow barn, pond and windmill, and watch the farm change with every task.

Grow crops and raise animals

Plant wheat, corn, carrots and strawberries; raise chickens, cows and bees; collect eggs, milk and honey.

Bake, cook and deliver

Build a bakery, dairy, jam kitchen and cake shop, turn the harvest into bread, cheese and cakes, and fill villagers’ orders for coins.

Home design

Step inside Grandma’s house and redecorate the living room, kitchen and bedroom — Classic, Modern or Cozy, and change it any time.

Quick tips

  • Leftover moves turn into bonus rockets at the end of a level — finish goals early when you can.
  • Combining two power-ups (Rocket + Bomb, Rainbow + anything) clears far more than using them alone.
  • Check daily quests: the bonus chest is the fastest way to stock hammers and shuffles.
