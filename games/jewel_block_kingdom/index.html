Jewel Block Kingdom

Drag jewel blocks onto an 8×8 board and clear full rows or columns. No timer, no pressure — and every game earns stars to build your own sparkling kingdom.

Get it on Google Play All TTGO apps

Simple to start, satisfying to master

Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing move after move, to chain big combos. Think ahead, plan your moves and beat your best score.

Three ways to play

Adventure mode with hundreds of gem-collecting levels and 3-star goals, endless Classic mode that saves automatically, and a Daily Challenge board that is the same for every player.

Build your kingdom

Spend stars on landmarks across 16 lands — from Crystal Village and Ruby Castle to Coral Reef and Star Station. Your kingdom earns coins every hour, even while you are away.

Boosters and styles

Hammer, Bomb (3×3) and Refresh help in tight spots. Pick gem, candy, neon, wood, ice or royal gold block skins and board themes.

Quick tips

  • Keep a 3×3 space open — it fits almost every piece.
  • Fill edges first and leave the middle flexible for awkward shapes.
  • Save the Bomb for boards with scattered single cells; the Hammer is better for one stray block.
