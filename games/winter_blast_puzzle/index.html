Tips to clear more winter levels

1. Read the objective before the first swap

Jumping straight into matches feels good, but wasted early moves are the most common reason stages fail. Spend two seconds on the goal strip. If you need blue blocks and ice removed, ignore a tempting red triple that does nothing for either goal. Prioritization is the difference between “almost cleared” and “level complete.”

2. Build toward the bottom and the center

Matches lower on the board often create longer cascades because more tiles fall through the cleared space. Center-board blasts can fan outward into multiple colors at once. Edge-only play can leave the middle locked under ice while you keep polishing the margins. Whenever two moves look equal, prefer the one that opens the densest region.

3. Save big blasts for congested boards

Special blasts and large matches are precious when blockers crowd the grid. Using a huge clear on an already open board feels spectacular and changes little. Holding that power for a packed ice cluster or a color that refuses to spawn can turn a failing board into a recovery. Patience is a strategy, not a slowdown.

4. Think in chains, not single matches

After every successful match, pause half a beat and scan what the drop created. Often the best second move is sitting one tile away from another blast. On move-limited stages, count what each remaining move must accomplish instead of tapping at random—Winter Blast Puzzle rewards readable planning over panic.
