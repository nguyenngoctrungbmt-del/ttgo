Blade Slash

Swipe to slice flying fruit, chain combos, and dodge the bombs.

▶ Play Blade Slash now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Blade Slash

  1. Swipe across the screen to slash fruit as it flies up.
  2. Slice 3+ fruit in one swipe for a combo bonus.
  3. Let a fruit fall and you lose a life. Slashing a bomb costs one too.
  4. Specials: ⭐ frenzy, ❄️ slow-mo, 💖 extra life. Hit the Golden Melon again and again. Upgrades add lives and luck.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Slice 30 fruit in one run
  • Land a 3-fruit combo
  • Slice 100 fruit in total
  • Score 80 in one run
  • Land a 4-fruit combo
  • Grab 5 specials (⭐ ❄️ 💖)

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Blade Slash:

  • ❤️ Thick Skin — +1 starting and max life
  • ⭐ Lucky Orchard — +30% chance of specials
  • 🪙 Bounty — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–3 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
