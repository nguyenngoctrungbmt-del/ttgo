Grapple Swing

Hook, swing, and fling yourself over lava and spinning saws.

▶ Play Grapple Swing now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Grapple Swing

  1. Hold anywhere to fire your grapple at the glowing hook ahead. Release to let go and fly.
  2. Swing builds speed — let go on the up-swing at speed for a PERFECT launch boost.
  3. Grab gems along the arcs. Lava, spinning saws and leaping fireballs end the run.
  4. The cave changes every 250 m. Upgrades add grapple range, a gem magnet and a lava shield.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Swing 200 m in one run
  • Land 3 perfect launches in one run
  • Collect 50 gems in total
  • Swing 500 m in one run
  • Land 10 perfect launches in one run
  • Collect 300 gems in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Grapple Swing:

  • 🪝 Long Line — +12% grapple range
  • 🧲 Gem Magnet — Pull nearby gems toward you
  • 🛡️ Ember Shield — Bounce off the lava once per run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–4 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
