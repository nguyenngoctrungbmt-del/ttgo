Bus Jam

Send each bus off with passengers of its colour — clear the queue.

▶ Play Bus Jam now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Bus Jam

  1. Tap a passenger with a clear path out of the crowd. Blocked passengers wait until the way opens up.
  2. Matching colours (and symbols) board the bus at the stop — 3 seats each. Full buses drive off and the next one pulls in.
  3. Wrong colour? They wait on the 5-seat bench and hop on when their bus comes. A full bench means you are out of space!
  4. VIP flies anyone out, Shuffle brings the right riders forward, Seat adds a bench seat. Watch for mystery, frozen, tunnel and VIP-pair passengers.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Board 150 passengers
  • Send off 12 buses in one run
  • Reach level 8
  • Clear 5 levels without the bench
  • Beat 2 hard levels

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Bus Jam:

  • 🚁 VIP Pass — +1 VIP pickup at the start of a run
  • 🪑 Long Bench — +1 bench seat on every level
  • 🪙 Fare Box — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
