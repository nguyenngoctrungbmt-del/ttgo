Word Nest

Guess the five-letter word in six tries with color clues.

▶ Play Word Nest now All games

How to play Word Nest

  1. Type a five-letter guess, then hit Enter to lock it in.
  2. Green = right letter, right spot. Yellow = right letter, wrong spot. Gray = not in the word.
  3. You have six tries each level. Win streak rises when you clear a word and resets if you fail.
  4. Stuck? Tap Skip level to jump to the next word.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
