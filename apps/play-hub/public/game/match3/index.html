Gem Match

Swap neighboring gems and clear runs of three or more.

▶ Play Gem Match now All games

How to play Gem Match

  1. Tap one gem, then an adjacent gem to swap them.
  2. Match three or more of the same color in a row or column.
  3. Gems fall and refill — chain clears for bigger scores.
  4. Clear the target before moves run out to advance.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
