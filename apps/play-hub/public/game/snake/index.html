Grid Snake

Slither, eat apples, and grow without hitting yourself.

▶ Play Grid Snake now All games

How to play Grid Snake

  1. Swipe or use arrow keys to steer the snake.
  2. Eat apples to grow and raise your score.
  3. Hitting a wall or your own body ends the run.
  4. Speed picks up as you clear more apples.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–3 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
