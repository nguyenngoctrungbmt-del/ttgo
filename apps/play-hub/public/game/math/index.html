How to play Quick Calc

  1. A simple math question appears with four answers.
  2. Tap the correct result before time runs out.
  3. Each correct answer grows your streak and score.
  4. Three wrong answers or timeouts end the round.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–2 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
