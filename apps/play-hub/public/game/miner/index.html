Gold Claw

Drop the swinging claw, haul up gold and beat the clock.

▶ Play Gold Claw now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Gold Claw

  1. The claw swings like a pendulum — tap to drop it. Heavy gold and rocks reel in slowly; diamonds are light and pricey.
  2. Earn each mine’s money target before the timer ends (30 hand-built mines, then endless). Tap Next once it is met for a time bonus.
  3. TNT barrels blow up everything nearby. Moles wander and some carry diamonds. Mystery bags hide cash, dynamite or strength.
  4. Spend your cash at the Trading Post between levels: dynamite (blast a bad catch), strength tonic, lucky clover and more.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Grab 5 diamonds
  • Earn $5,000 in total
  • Reach level 6
  • Catch 10 moles
  • Use 10 sticks of dynamite

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Gold Claw:

  • 💪 Strong Arms — +10% reel speed
  • ⏱️ Overtime — +5 seconds every level
  • 🧨 Demo Kit — Start with +1 dynamite

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
