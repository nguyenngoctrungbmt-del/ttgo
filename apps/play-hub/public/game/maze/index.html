Grid Maze

Navigate from S to E through shrinking corridors.

▶ Play Grid Maze now All games

How to play Grid Maze

  1. You start at S. Reach the E tile.
  2. Swipe on the board or use keyboard arrows to move.
  3. Walls block movement — plan a short path.
  4. Clear larger mazes for a bigger score.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–4 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
