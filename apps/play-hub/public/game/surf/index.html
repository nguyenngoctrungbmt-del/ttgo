Wave Surfer

Pump the wave face, flip off the lip, and outrun the closing curl.

▶ Play Wave Surfer now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Wave Surfer

  1. Hold to carve up the wave face, release to drop. Dropping down the face builds speed — pump up and down to go faster.
  2. Hit the lip with speed to launch. Hold in the air to flip, let go to straighten, and land upright for big points.
  3. The breaking curl chases you — if it catches you, the run ends. Rocks, buoys, surfers, sharks, gulls and, further out, whirlpools, jet-skis, diving pelicans and the Kraken cost a board.
  4. Ride just ahead of the curl for a BARREL bonus. Grab coins, pearls, shields, turbo, magnets and stars; upgrade Spare Board, Pro Fins and Grip Wax.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Surf 500 m in one run
  • Land 5 flips in total
  • Collect 100 coins in total
  • Surf 1,500 m in one run
  • Ride 3 barrels
  • Land 5 flips in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Wave Surfer:

  • 🏄 Spare Board — +1 board (survive one more wipeout)
  • 🦈 Pro Fins — +6% speed and pumping power
  • 🧴 Grip Wax — Easier landings, +15% trick points

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
