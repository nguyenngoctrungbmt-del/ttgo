Lumber Chop

Chop left, chop right, dodge branches — and beat the clock.

▶ Play Lumber Chop now All games

How to play Lumber Chop

  1. Tap the left or right half of the screen to jump to that side and chop the trunk.
  2. Never stand under a branch — a branch on your side, or one that drops onto you, ends the run.
  3. The timer drains faster every level; every chop refills it. Chop fast to get ON FIRE for double points.
  4. Golden logs pay big, Axe Fury smashes branches, Freeze stops the timer, iron logs need 2 chops. Upgrades add helmets, stamina and gold.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Chop 50 logs in one run
  • Chop 5 golden logs
  • Chop 500 logs in total
  • Chop 150 logs in one run
  • Use 15 power-up logs
  • Reach level 6

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Lumber Chop:

  • ⏳ Stamina — Timer drains 8% slower
  • ⛑️ Hard Hat — Survive 1 more branch hit per run
  • ✨ Golden Touch — More golden logs, +15% coins

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
