Goods Sort

Match three goods on a shelf to clear the store.

▶ Play Goods Sort now All games

How to play Goods Sort

  1. Drag a good (or tap it, then tap a shelf) into an empty slot on another shelf.
  2. Three identical goods on one shelf pop. When a shelf front empties, the goods behind slide forward.
  3. Clear every shelf before the timer runs out — no free slot left means you are out of space. Quick clears build combos.
  4. Locked shelves open after enough clears; frozen goods thaw as you clear. Magnet, Shuffle and Freeze boosters help — upgrades add more.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Pop 60 triples in total
  • Hit a x3 combo
  • Reach level 7
  • Earn 15 stars in one run
  • Pop 800 triples in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Goods Sort:

  • 🧲 Magnet Kit — +1 Magnet at the start of a run
  • ⏱️ Long Hours — +8 seconds on every level timer
  • 🪙 Shop Profits — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
