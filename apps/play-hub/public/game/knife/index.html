Knife Toss

Throw knives into a spinning target — just never hit another blade.

▶ Play Knife Toss now All games

How to play Knife Toss

  1. Tap to throw a knife straight up into the spinning target.
  2. Stick every knife to clear the stage — the target bursts apart. Hitting a knife already stuck ends the run.
  3. Slice 🍎 apples on the rim for bonus points.
  4. Spin patterns get trickier, and every 5th stage is a boss target with surges and reversals.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach stage 4
  • Slice 10 apples
  • Stick 100 knives in total
  • Beat a boss target
  • Reach stage 8
  • Slice 50 apples

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Knife Toss:

  • 🛡️ Guardian — Forgive 1 knife collision per run
  • 🍎 Orchard — More apples on every target
  • 🪙 Bounty — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–4 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
