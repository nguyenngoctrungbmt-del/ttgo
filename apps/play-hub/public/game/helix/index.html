Helix Drop

Spin the tower, drop through gaps and smash rings as a fireball.

▶ Play Helix Drop now All games

How to play Helix Drop

  1. Drag left or right to spin the tower. The ball bounces on its own — line up a gap to drop through.
  2. Reach the golden pad to clear the level: 30 hand-built towers, then endless ones. Stars: clear it, take no red hit, grab every gem.
  3. Red segments are deadly. Later rings spin by themselves and some red blinks on and off — watch the flicker.
  4. Fall through 3 rings in a row to become a fireball that smashes the next ring, even red. Collect gems; upgrades add shields and bigger smashes.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Drop through 100 rings
  • Smash 10 rings as a fireball
  • Reach level 6
  • Collect 50 gems
  • Pass 120 rings in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Helix Drop:

  • 🛡️ Bubble Shield — Survive 1 more red hit per run
  • 🔥 Blaze — Fireball smashes +1 extra ring
  • 💎 Gem Hunter — More gems, +15% coins

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
