Goal Keeper

Dive, parry and catch curlers, dippers and rockets in goal.

▶ Play Goal Keeper now All games

How to play Goal Keeper

  1. Drag anywhere to move your gloves — tap inside the goal to snap them there. The keeper dives on his own.
  2. Watch the striker and the shrinking target ring, then get your gloves on the ball. Dead-centre stops are clean catches.
  3. Every goal you concede costs a life. Strikers learn curlers, dippers, power shots, knuckle balls, fake-outs and double balls.
  4. Saves build a streak multiplier and fill FOCUS — tap it for slow motion. Upgrades add lives, bigger gloves and sharper reads.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Make 10 saves in one game
  • Catch 5 shots cleanly
  • Make 100 saves in total
  • Reach round 5
  • Save 12 shots in a row
  • Beat a legend striker

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Goal Keeper:

  • ❤️ Iron Wall — +1 life every game
  • 🧤 Big Gloves — +12% save radius
  • 👁️ Sharp Eyes — Target ring shows earlier, FOCUS starts charged

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
