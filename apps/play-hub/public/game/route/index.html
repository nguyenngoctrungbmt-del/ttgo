Route Recall

Memorise the directions, then drive the route turn by turn.

▶ Play Route Recall now All games

How to play Route Recall

  1. Study the turn-by-turn directions before your fare starts — tap to start driving early.
  2. The taxi drives itself. Before each junction tap LEFT, AHEAD or RIGHT; it waits for you, so take your time.
  3. A wrong turn costs a heart and replays the remaining directions. Later fares use landmarks: "Left at the Cafe".
  4. Tap the GPS pin to peek at the route. Fast fares earn a speed bonus, and every 5th fare is a VIP ride.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach fare 4
  • Make 40 correct turns
  • Drive 3 perfect fares in a run
  • Reach fare 10
  • Earn 10 speed bonuses
  • Make 400 correct turns

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Route Recall:

  • 🗺️ Route Planner — +15% time to study directions
  • 📍 GPS Peek — Start each run with +1 route peek
  • 🛞 Spare Tire — +1 wrong turn forgiven per run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
