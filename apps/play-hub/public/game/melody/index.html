Melody Keys

Listen to the tune, then play it back on the keys.

▶ Play Melody Keys now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Melody Keys

  1. The songbird sings a short tune while the xylophone keys light up — each key has its own colour and symbol.
  2. Tap the keys to play the tune back in the same order. Finish it with no mistakes for a perfect streak.
  3. A wrong key costs a heart and the tune plays again. Tunes grow longer and faster, with repeats, rhythm and more keys.
  4. Tap the replay button to hear it again. Every 5th level is an Echo bonus — later, play backwards or follow shuffled keys.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 4
  • Play 100 correct notes
  • Play 3 perfect tunes in a row
  • Reach level 10
  • Echo 8 notes in a bonus round
  • Play 1,000 correct notes

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Melody Keys:

  • 🐢 Easy Tempo — Tunes play 8% slower
  • 👂 Good Ear — Start each run with +1 replay
  • ✨ Guide Light — One more opening note glows as a hint

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
