Last Stand

Hold the barricade — headshot the horde before it breaks through.

▶ Play Last Stand now All games

How to play Last Stand

  1. Tap a zombie to shoot it. Headshots kill instantly and score double.
  2. You have 8 rounds — it reloads automatically when empty, or tap the ammo bar to reload early.
  3. Runners, crawlers and brutes smash the wall; later spitters lob acid and bombers explode. Bosses every 5 waves.
  4. Tap dropped items: grenades clear the area, stopwatches slow time, wrenches repair the barricade.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Take down 25 zombies in one run
  • Land 10 headshots in one run
  • Take down 200 zombies in total
  • Reach wave 5
  • Chain 5 headshots in a row
  • Score 2,000 in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Last Stand:

  • 🔫 Extended Mag — +2 rounds per magazine
  • 🧱 Reinforced Wall — +20 max barricade
  • 🍀 Scavenger — Zombies drop items more often

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
