Basket Flick

Flick swishes into a sliding hoop and catch fire before time runs out.

▶ Play Basket Flick now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Basket Flick

  1. Swipe up from the ball to shoot: the direction aims, the swipe length sets the power.
  2. Every basket adds time to the clock. Swishes (no rim) add more — the run ends when time hits zero.
  3. Three swishes in a row sets you ON FIRE for double points. Gold balls are worth triple.
  4. Each level the hoop gets trickier: it slides, bobs, shrinks and the wind picks up. Upgrades add time, aim guides and longer fire.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Score 10 baskets in one game
  • Hit 10 swishes
  • Score 100 baskets in total
  • Reach level 5
  • Catch fire 3 times
  • Score 10 baskets in a row

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Basket Flick:

  • ⏱️ Overtime — +4 s on the starting clock
  • 🎯 Aim Guide — Longer trajectory preview while aiming
  • 🔥 Hot Hand — Fire mode lasts 3 s longer

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
