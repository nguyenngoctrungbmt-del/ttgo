Slope Ski

Carve downhill, spin off ramps, and outrun the hungry yeti.

▶ Play Slope Ski now All games

How to play Slope Ski

  1. Touch and drag: your skis point toward your finger. Straight down is fastest, sideways scrubs speed.
  2. Pass between slalom flags for combo points. Hit ramps, then swipe left/right in the air to spin and tap to grab — land straight!
  3. Trees, rocks, cabins and other skiers cost a heart. Past 1,200 m the yeti hunts you — ski straight to outrun it.
  4. Collect coins for upgrades: Helmet (+1 heart), Race Wax (faster) and Stunt Pro (easier landings, more points).

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Ski 600 m in one run
  • Pass 20 slalom gates
  • Land 8 tricks in total
  • Ski 1,600 m in one run
  • Escape the yeti
  • Land 5 tricks in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Slope Ski:

  • ⛑️ Helmet — +1 heart at the start of every run
  • 🎿 Race Wax — +5% top speed (outrun the yeti)
  • 🌀 Stunt Pro — Wider landing window, +15% trick points

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
