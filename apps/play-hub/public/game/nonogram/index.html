Dot Grid

Paint cells from the number clues — classic picross.

▶ Play Dot Grid now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Dot Grid

  1. Row and column numbers show runs of filled cells.
  2. Tap a cell to fill it; long-press (or use Mark) to X it out.
  3. Match the full picture to clear the puzzle.
  4. Boards grow from 5×5 up to 10×10 across stages.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 5–15 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
