Glass Smash

Fly down a glass corridor, smashing panes to keep moving.

▶ Play Glass Smash now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Glass Smash

  1. You fly forward on your own. Tap anywhere to throw a ball at that spot — every throw costs 1 ball.
  2. Smash any glass in your path. Flying into glass costs 10 balls; metal frames just block your throws.
  3. Hit the blue crystals for +3 balls (big ones +5). Chain hits without missing to throw 2, 3 or 4 balls at once.
  4. Break the gate at the end of each room to move on — rooms get faster and trickier. Run out of balls and the run ends.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Fly 300 m in one run
  • Shatter 100 panes
  • Collect 30 crystals
  • Reach room 5
  • Hit a 15-hit streak
  • Fly 1,000 m in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Glass Smash:

  • ⚪ Deep Pockets — +5 balls at the start of every run
  • 💎 Crystal Lens — Crystals give +1 extra ball
  • 🛡️ Safety Glass — Crashes cost 2 fewer balls

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
