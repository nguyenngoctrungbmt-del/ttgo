City Shield

Intercept falling missiles and set off chain-reaction blasts.

▶ Play City Shield now Get the app on Google Play

How to play City Shield

  1. Tap the sky to fire an interceptor from the nearest battery. It explodes where you tapped.
  2. Missiles caught in a blast explode too — chain them for big points. Lose every city and the run ends.
  3. Batteries hold 10 shots per wave. Shoot supply crates for ammo; beware MIRVs, bombers and smart bombs.
  4. Every 5th wave a mothership attacks — hit it 3 times. Upgrades add ammo, blast size and shields.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Destroy 30 missiles in one run
  • Reach wave 3
  • Destroy 200 missiles in total
  • Set off a 4-missile chain
  • Reach wave 6
  • Finish 3 waves without losing a city

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for City Shield:

  • 🚀 Deep Magazines — +2 shots per battery each wave
  • 💥 Bigger Warheads — +10% interceptor blast radius
  • 🛡️ Shield Dome — Absorb 1 city hit per run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
