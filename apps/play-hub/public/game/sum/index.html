Sum Duo

Pick two numbers that add up to the target.

How to play Sum Duo

  1. A target sum appears at the top.
  2. Tap two numbers on the board that add up to it.
  3. Correct pairs raise your streak and score.
  4. Three wrong pairs end the round.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–2 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
