Crane Tower

Drop swinging floors from the crane and build the tallest tower.

▶ Play Crane Tower now All games

How to play Crane Tower

  1. Tap anywhere to drop the floor swinging from the crane onto your tower.
  2. Overhangs get trimmed off and make the tower sway; PERFECT drops keep the width, chain combos and move more residents in.
  3. A missed floor costs a hard hat. Lose them all, or let the SWAY meter fill, and the tower topples.
  4. Watch for wind gusts, heavy concrete and a drifting trolley. Pick a site perk every 10 floors; gardens calm the tower.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Build a 10-floor tower
  • Land 15 perfect drops
  • House 300 residents
  • Build a 25-floor tower
  • Chain 6 perfect drops
  • Place 6 rooftop gardens

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Crane Tower:

  • 🧲 Steady Crane — Smaller swing and 10% less sway per level
  • ⛑️ Hard Hats — +1 hard hat (missed floor) per run
  • 📐 Laser Guide — Wider PERFECT window

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
