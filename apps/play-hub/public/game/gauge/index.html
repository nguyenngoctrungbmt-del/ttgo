Perfect Stop

Stop the sliding marker inside the green zone.

▶ Play Perfect Stop now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Perfect Stop

  1. Watch the marker bounce across the track.
  2. Tap Stop when it sits inside the green zone.
  3. Closer to the center earns more points.
  4. The zone shrinks and speed rises each level.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–2 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
