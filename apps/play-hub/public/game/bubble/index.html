Bubble Blast

Bank shots off the walls, pop trios and drop whole clusters.

▶ Play Bubble Blast now All games

How to play Bubble Blast

  1. Drag to aim — the dotted guide bounces off the walls — and release to shoot. Tap the dish on the left to swap bubbles.
  2. Connect 3 or more of one colour to pop them. Anything left hanging falls. Clear the whole board within the shots to win the level.
  3. 32 hand-drawn boards, a boss board every 5th. On some boards the ceiling drops every few shots (watch the pips); crossing the red line ends the run.
  4. Pops fill the fever ring for rainbow, bomb and lightning bubbles; stars give extra fever. Upgrades extend the guide, speed up fever and slow the pushes.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Pop 150 bubbles
  • Reach level 3
  • Drop 60 bubbles
  • Reach level 6
  • Pop on 6 shots in a row
  • Use 20 special bubbles

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Bubble Blast:

  • 🎯 Laser Sight — Longer guide with more bounces; landing ghost at Lv 2
  • 🔥 Hot Streak — Fever ring fills 20% faster
  • ⏳ Breather — +1 shot before each ceiling drop

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
