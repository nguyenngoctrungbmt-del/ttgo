Flash Reflex

Wait for green, then tap as fast as you can.

How to play Flash Reflex

  1. Tap Start and wait. The pad stays warm orange while waiting.
  2. When it flashes green, tap immediately.
  3. Tap too early and that try is void — stay patient.
  4. Best (lowest) reaction time is saved as your score.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–2 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
