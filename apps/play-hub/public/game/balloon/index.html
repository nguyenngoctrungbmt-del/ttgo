Balloon Pop

How to play Balloon Pop

  1. Watch the target color shown at the top.
  2. Pop balloons that match the target color.
  3. Wrong pops cost a life. Missed targets also hurt.
  4. Survive 30 seconds and stack combos for a high score.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 1–2 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
