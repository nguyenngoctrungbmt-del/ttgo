Sand Blast

Drop sand blocks and bridge a colour from wall to wall.

▶ Play Sand Blast now All games

How to play Sand Blast

  1. Drag to slide the falling block, tap to rotate it, swipe down to drop it. On landing it crumbles into sand that trickles and piles up.
  2. Connect one colour of sand from the left wall to the right wall — the whole bridge flashes and blasts away. Chains score extra.
  3. Build the target number of bridges to clear the level. If the sand piles past the red line, it overflows and the run ends.
  4. Bomb blows a crater, Rainbow sand matches any colour, Swap trades for the next block. Grey stones never clear. Upgrades add bombs, calmer sand and coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Blast 10 sand bridges
  • Chain 2 bridges in a row
  • Reach level 8
  • Blast 100 sand bridges
  • Beat 2 hard levels

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Sand Blast:

  • 💣 Demolition — +1 bomb at the start of a run
  • 🌬️ Calm Winds — Blocks fall 8% slower
  • 🪙 Gold Dust — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
