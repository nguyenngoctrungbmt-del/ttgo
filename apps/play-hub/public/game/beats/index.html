Beat Tapper

Tap four lanes to fresh synth tracks — every song a little faster.

▶ Play Beat Tapper now All games

How to play Beat Tapper

  1. Tap a lane (or press D F J K) when its note reaches the glowing line. Keep holding long notes until their tail ends; tap both lanes for linked double notes.
  2. Timing scores Perfect, Great or Good. Chain hits for a combo multiplier up to ×4; Perfects fill the fever meter for double points.
  3. Misses drain your health bar — when it empties the show is over. Hits and song clears heal you.
  4. Songs are generated live and play back to back, faster each time, in new styles. Upgrades soften misses, widen timing and extend fever.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Clear your first song
  • Hit a 30-note combo
  • Land 200 Perfect hits
  • Clear 3 songs in one run
  • Trigger Fever 10 times
  • Hit a 100-note combo

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Beat Tapper:

  • ❤️ Thick Skin — Misses drain 12% less health
  • 🎯 Timing Assist — Hit windows 10% wider
  • 🔥 Fever Rush — Fever lasts 1.5s longer, +5% coins

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
