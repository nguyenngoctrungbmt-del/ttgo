Car Merge

Merge buggies into rocket cars and race them to beat every season.

▶ Play Car Merge now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Car Merge

  1. Drag a car onto an identical one to merge it into a faster, pricier model: buggy, hatchback, sedan… up to the rocket car.
  2. Cars in the top row (pit lanes) race the oval and earn cash every lap. Drag cars between the garage and the pit lanes; drop one on Buy to sell it.
  3. Each 60-second season has a cash target — miss it and the run ends. Targets rise, new pit lanes open and every 5th season is a Grand Prix.
  4. Tap the track for nitro, catch golden cars for gifts, and ride sponsor rushes. Coins buy a pit crew, engine tuning and sponsor bonuses.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Clear 3 seasons
  • Merge 30 cars
  • Build a Muscle Car
  • Clear 6 seasons
  • Drive 2,000 laps
  • Build a Supercar

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Car Merge:

  • 🔧 Pit Crew — Start with an extra Hatchback
  • ⚙️ Engine Tuning — +8% speed for every car
  • 🪙 Sponsor — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
