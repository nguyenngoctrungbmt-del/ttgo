Bridge Builder

Lay beams across the gap so the traffic makes it over.

▶ Play Bridge Builder now All games

How to play Bridge Builder

  1. Drag from a red anchor or a joint to place a beam; tap a beam to remove it. Road carries wheels, wood and steel hold it up.
  2. Press GO: the bridge settles, then traffic drives across. Beams glow green to red under load and snap when overloaded.
  3. Get every vehicle to the flag. Spend less of the budget for more stars; a collapse costs a retry.
  4. 32 hand-made sites: cliffs, ship lanes, wind, trucks and buses, a boss crossing every 5th. Pick a site perk every 4 levels.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 4
  • Earn 15 stars
  • Open 10 bridges
  • Reach level 10
  • Build 10 three-star bridges
  • Open 6 bridges in a row, no fails

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Bridge Builder:

  • 💰 City Funding — +6% budget on every level
  • 🔩 Tough Materials — All beams 6% stronger
  • 🔧 Spare Crew — +1 retry per run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
