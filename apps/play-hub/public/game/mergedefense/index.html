Merge Defenders

Summon and merge archers, mages and cannons to hold the road.

▶ Play Merge Defenders now All games

How to play Merge Defenders

  1. Tap Summon to place a random tier 1 defender: Archer, Mage, Cannon, Frost or Storm. Each summon costs a little more; kills and cleared waves pay coins.
  2. Drag a defender onto an identical one (same type and tier) to merge them into a random defender one tier higher. Drag onto any other slot to move it.
  3. Monsters march along the road. Each one that reaches the castle costs a heart (bosses cost two) — lose them all and the run ends.
  4. Every 5th wave brings a mini-boss and every 10th a boss. Power buttons boost a whole unit type. Upgrades add hearts, starting coins and damage.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach wave 5
  • Merge 30 defenders in total
  • Make a tier 3 defender
  • Defeat a boss
  • Make a tier 5 defender
  • Defeat 2,000 monsters in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Merge Defenders:

  • ❤️ Castle Walls — +1 heart at the start of a run
  • 🪙 War Chest — +25 starting coins
  • ⚔️ Sharpened Steel — +8% damage for every defender

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 4–12 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
