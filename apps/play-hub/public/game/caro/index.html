Caro

Classic five-in-a-row duel against a crafty CPU.

▶ Play Caro now All games

How to play Caro

  1. You play X. Tap an empty cell to place your mark.
  2. Get five in a row — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal — to win.
  3. Block the CPU while building your own line.
  4. Clear the board without five-in-a-row for a draw.

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 3–10 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
