Pulse Dash

Auto-run to the beat — jump spikes, hit orbs and flip gravity.

▶ Play Pulse Dash now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Pulse Dash

  1. Your cube runs by itself. Tap or hold to jump; holding keeps jumping as soon as you land.
  2. Spikes and the sides of blocks are deadly — land on top of blocks. Falling into a gap ends the run.
  3. Yellow pads launch you high, tap yellow orbs in mid-air to jump again, portals flip gravity or turn you into a ship (hold to rise).
  4. 30 hand-built stages teach and remix each mechanic (a boss stage every 5th), then an endless remix. Stars: clear, no Guardian save, every gem.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach stage 2
  • Collect 30 gems
  • Jump 300 times
  • Reach stage 4
  • Hit 40 jump orbs
  • Clear 3 ship sections

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Pulse Dash:

  • 🛡️ Guardian — Survive +1 crash per run
  • 💎 Gem Magnet — Grab gems from further away
  • 🪙 Bounty — +20% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
