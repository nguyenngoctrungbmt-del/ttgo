What Changed?

Study a cosy little scene, blink, and spot what is different.

▶ Play What Changed? now All games

How to play What Changed?

  1. Study the scene — a room, a park, an aquarium or a desk — until the eyes blink shut.
  2. When they open, something has changed: an object moved, vanished, appeared, changed colour or turned. Tap where it happened.
  3. A wrong tap costs a heart. Quick, perfect rounds build a streak multiplier. Later rounds hide 2–3 changes in busier scenes.
  4. Peek shows the original scene again; Hint marks one change. Upgrades add study time, hearts and power-ups.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 5
  • Spot 25 changes in total
  • Play 8 perfect rounds
  • Reach level 10
  • Get a 5-round perfect streak
  • Spot 250 changes in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for What Changed?:

  • ⏳ Long Look — +0.5 s study time every round
  • ❤️ Big Heart — +1 max heart
  • 🔍 Detective Kit — +1 starting Peek and Hint

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
