Arrow Escape

Tap arrows to fly them off the grid — don't let them collide.

▶ Play Arrow Escape now Get the app on Google Play

How to play Arrow Escape

  1. Tap an arrow to launch it the way its head points. Long arrows slither after their head like a snake.
  2. If nothing is in front of the head, the arrow flies off the grid. If something blocks it, it bumps back and you lose a heart.
  3. Clear every arrow to finish the level. You have 3 hearts per level — every 5th level is a packed hard grid, and stones never move.
  4. Hint lights up a safe arrow, Hammer smashes one, Shield absorbs the next bump. Upgrades add hearts, hints and coins.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Reach level 3
  • Launch 150 arrows to freedom
  • Clear a level without a bump
  • Reach level 8
  • Beat 2 hard levels
  • Free 1,500 arrows in total

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Arrow Escape:

  • ❤️ Extra Heart — +1 heart on every level
  • 💡 Bright Idea — +1 hint at the start of a run
  • 🪙 Quiver Gold — +15% coins from each run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–5 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
