Road Rush

Draw roads so every car gets to work on time.

▶ Play Road Rush now All games

How to play Road Rush

  1. Drag across the grid to lay road tiles. Houses send their cars along roads to the workplace of the same colour.
  2. Workplaces collect demand pins. More than six waiting starts a red timer; if it fills, the run ends.
  3. Road tiles are limited: plan short routes and avoid jams, since busy junctions slow cars down. Erase gives tiles back.
  4. Every week brings new houses, colours and an upgrade: bridges for water, motorways, roundabouts, extra cars. Upgrades add tiles, patience and speed.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Complete 50 trips in one run
  • Reach week 3
  • Complete 500 trips in total
  • Reach week 6
  • Complete 250 trips in one run
  • Build 12 bridges

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Road Rush:

  • 🛣️ Road Budget — +6 starting road tiles
  • ⏳ Patient Bosses — Workplaces wait 15% longer
  • 🚗 Zippy Cars — Cars drive 6% faster

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 4–8 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
