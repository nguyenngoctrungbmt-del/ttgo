Neon Racer

Weave through night traffic, skim cars for nitro, outrun the cops.

▶ Play Neon Racer now All games

How to play Neon Racer

  1. Drag left or right anywhere to steer (arrow keys on desktop). Your car speeds up the farther you go.
  2. Pass cars as close as you can: near misses build a combo and fill the NITRO ring — tap it to boost and smash through traffic.
  3. Watch blinkers, road works, oil slicks and police chases; crashing costs armor, and with no armor left the run ends.
  4. Grab coins, nitro cans, armor and magnets. Spend coins on Armor, Turbo Tank and Coin Magnet upgrades.

Missions to chase

Every run counts toward missions that pay out XP. Clear a tier to unlock the next one:

  • Drive 800 m in one run
  • Get 25 near misses
  • Collect 100 coins in total
  • Drive 2,500 m in one run
  • Escape a police chase
  • Get 30 near misses in one run

Upgrades

Coins you earn buy permanent upgrades for Neon Racer:

  • 🛡️ Armor Plating — +1 armor (survive one more crash)
  • ⚡ Turbo Tank — Start with +30% nitro; boosts last 15% longer
  • 🧲 Coin Magnet — Wider coin pull and +10% coins per run

Good to know

  • Free and instant: runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop — nothing to install.
  • Session length: about 2–6 min per round, perfect for a short break.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser: best scores, missions and favorites.
