Jewel Block Kingdom: 8 Tips to Clear More Lines and Build Faster

An 8×8 board, three pieces at a time, no timer. Block puzzles are easy to learn and surprisingly deep — here is how to stay alive longer and chain bigger combos.

Published 2026-10-25 · By Trung Nguyen, TTGO / AVTFINITY

8 tips for higher scores

  1. Always keep a 3×3 space open. It is the one shape that accepts almost every piece, including the big square.
  2. Plan for all three pieces. Before placing the first, check that the other two will still fit afterwards.
  3. Fill edges and corners first. Keep the middle flexible for awkward L and line pieces.
  4. Avoid single-cell holes. Only the 1×1 piece can fill them — and it rarely comes when you need it.
  5. Clear several lines at once. Multi-line clears and back-to-back clears build combos that score far more than single lines.
  6. Hammer for one block, Bomb for a mess. The Hammer smashes a single block; the Bomb clears a 3×3 area full of scattered cells.
  7. Refresh only when stuck. It gives you three new pieces — use it when none of the current ones fit.
  8. Take your time. There is no timer. A five-second look at the board beats a fast mistake.

Pick the right mode

  • Adventure: hundreds of levels — collect the gems hidden in the blocks and aim for 3 stars.
  • Classic: the endless board, saved automatically so you can stop and continue anytime.
  • Daily Challenge: one fresh board a day, the same for every player. Keep the streak alive for monthly rewards.

Grow your kingdom

Every game earns stars. Spend them on landmarks across 16 lands — from Crystal Village and Ruby Castle to Coral Reef and Star Station. Your kingdom earns coins every hour even while you are away, and finishing a land opens a reward chest and the next land. The shop also has block skins — gem, candy, neon, wood, ice and royal gold — plus board themes.
