PlayVerse vs 100 Offline Game No Wifi: Which Puzzle Collection Is for You?

Both apps put many puzzles in one install, but they are built for different players. Here is a quick side-by-side so you can pick the right one — or both.

Published 2026-10-25 · By Trung Nguyen, TTGO / AVTFINITY

PlayVerse: depth and a daily habit

PlayVerse leans into proper logic puzzles — Nonograms (Picross), Slitherlink, Hitori, Fillomino next to Sudoku and Minesweeper — plus tile games like Mahjong Solitaire and Tile Master, merge games such as 2048 and Merge Dice, and board classics Connect 4 and Reversi against the AI. A Daily Challenge and daily missions give you a reason to open it every day.

Screenshots from the PlayVerse Google Play listing.

100 Offline Game No Wifi: maximum variety

100 Offline Game No Wifi is about breadth: 100+ puzzles in one install, made for flights, subways and weak signal. It covers block and hexagon puzzles, sudoku, tile matching (including mahjong-style boards), tangram, number merge, ball sort and word search, so you can switch genres without downloading anything else.

100 Offline Games No Wifi – Mahjong, Water Sort, Sudoku & 2048 in One App — from the TTGO Game Channel.

Our recommendation

  • You love Sudoku, Nonograms or Mahjong and want a daily routine → PlayVerse.
  • You want the most games for a trip with no signal → 100 Offline Game No Wifi.
  • Can’t decide? Both are free — install both and keep the one you open more.
