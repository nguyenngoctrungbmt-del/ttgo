Who makes the games

TTGO games are designed and built by Trung Nguyen, a Lead Unity Developer, under the AVTFINITY publisher account — from game design and code to the store pages you see on Google Play. The browser games in the Play Hub are web versions of our Today Puzzle app, so they load in seconds on any phone or computer. You can watch a short video introduction on the CV page, and follow new trailers on our YouTube channel.

What we care about

  • Short sessions. A level should fit a coffee break. Every game shows its goal on the first screen.
  • Offline first. Most of our Android games keep working on planes, subways and in weak signal.
  • Readable boards. Clear colours and friendly themes — cats, jewels, snow — so you never misread a tile.
  • No accounts. Install and play. Progress saves on your device.

Play before you install

Not sure an app is for you? The TTGO Play Hub has 140+ games from Today Puzzle that run right in the browser — Bus Jam, Fruit Drop, Hexa Sort, Blade Slash, Word Nest and many more. If you like one, the Android app gives you the same games offline, with daily puzzles and rewards.
