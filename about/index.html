About TTGO

Last updated: September 18, 2026

TTGO is the consumer brand for free puzzle and casual games published by AVTFINITY. This website—ttgo.io.vn—is our public home for instant browser play and for Android titles listed on Google Play.

What we offer

We build small, focused games you can start in seconds: merge puzzles, classic board games (Connect 4, Reversi, Chess, Caro), memory and reflex titles, and action pick-ups like Flappy Bird and Snake. On Android we publish longer-form puzzle apps—block puzzle, water sort, match-blast, mahjong match, and offline collections—so people can play without relying on a constant connection.

  • Browser games on TTGO Play — no install, no account required
  • Android apps catalog — Play Store downloads with landing pages and FAQs
  • Blog — notes on puzzle genres, offline play, and how our titles fit casual gaming

How we work

Our goal is useful entertainment: clear rules, readable boards, and sessions that fit a break or a commute. We maintain this site with updated game pages, privacy and terms information, and links to official Google Play listings so players can verify the publisher before installing.

Advertising helps keep browser games free. We use Google AdSense where appropriate and explain cookies and data practices on our Privacy Policy page.
